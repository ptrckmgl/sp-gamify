How XP works
  • Task +10 XP, subtask +5 XP
  • +1 XP per 5 min tracked (max 48)
  • +15 XP when finished within estimate
  • +10 XP when finished before the due date
  • Habit done today +10 XP, +20 bonus every 7-day habit streak
  • −10 XP per overdue task and per missed habit day
  • Main streak grows each day you reach your goal; habit streaks grow each day you do the habit
  • Finish tasks within 30 min of each other: combo bonus from the 3rd task (+5, +10, +15, max +20)
  • Daily quests: +15 XP each, +15 more when all three are done
  • A streak freeze covers a missed day. Earn one every 7 streak days (hold up to 2)
  • Unchecking a task or habit takes its XP back
